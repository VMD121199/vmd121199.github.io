I'm an AI Research Engineer at L2TI Laboratory (LabCom IRISER, ANR) in Paris, where I work on next-generation object detection for aerial and remote sensing imagery.

My research focuses on efficiency and accuracy — designing benchmarks, optimizing neural networks via pruning and quantization, and pushing Spiking Neural Networks for energy-efficient inference on embedded systems.

Before research, I spent two years as a Blockchain Developer building DeFi and GameFi products at scale, and interned as a Computer Vision Engineer across two companies working with YOLO, SSD, and VGG architectures. That cross-domain experience gives me a uniquely practical perspective on AI systems.
